# Open Research: Data and Software Availability

## Statement to use before the Zenodo DOI is issued

Level-2 Magnetospheric Multiscale (MMS) magnetic-field, electric-field,
particle, and ephemeris data used in this study were obtained from the
[NASA Space Physics Data Facility (SPDF)](https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/mms/).
The analysis uses MMS1-MMS4 observations from 22 January 2016, with a principal
burst-mode interval of 07:18:44-07:18:53 UT and a coordinate-analysis interval
of 07:18:36.860-07:19:00.920 UT. The products include FGM burst magnetic
fields, EDP burst electric fields, FPI/DIS and FPI/DES Level-2 moments,
FPI/DIS ion velocity distributions, and MEC ephemeris data. Exact source-file
names, versions, product directories, and direct SPDF download links are listed
in the
[source-data manifest](https://github.com/dipsbc/MMS-Tripolar-Hall-Field-20160122/blob/main/metadata/MMS_source_files.md).
The analysis notebook, processing metadata, derived figure-source data, and
figure-production materials are available from the
[project GitHub repository](https://github.com/dipsbc/MMS-Tripolar-Hall-Field-20160122).
A permanent versioned archive and DOI will be provided through Zenodo before
acceptance of the manuscript.
